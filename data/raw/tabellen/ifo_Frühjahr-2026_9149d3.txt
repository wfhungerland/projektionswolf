 The ifo Institute publishes an economic forecast four times a year. The focus is on forecasting overall economic development in Germany for a period of up to two years. Due to the close interdependence of the German economy with the global economy, and in particular with the European economy, developments in the European Union and other important countries are also forecast. The forecast is based on a thorough analysis of the economic situation. 
 The data used for the economic forecast and the forecast values are available for free download here. 
 Three versions were calculated in the spring 2026 forecast: 
 
 one version dates from before the start of the war in the Gulf region (still without sector accounting), 
 one version assumes a de-escalation of the war, 
 and one version assumes an escalation of the war. 
 
 The data is licensed under the Creative Commons licence CC-BY-4.0.
